About this project

A secondhand watch value tracker built with Python and Flask.

What it does

Watch Tracker follows the secondhand market value of six reference watches from January 2022 to September 2026. For each watch, it shows the current price, its 12-month change, its premium over retail price and its volatility, along with a price history chart.

The simulator answers a simple question: if you had bought a given watch in a given month at a given price, what would it be worth today?

How it works

  • Flask handles the routes, the templates and the form.
  • pandas loads and filters the price history.
  • NumPy computes the returns, price changes and annualized volatility.
  • Matplotlib draws the price charts, generated on each visit.

About the data

All prices on this site are simulated for demonstration purposes. They follow the general shape of the luxury watch market since 2022, a peak in early 2022 followed by a correction through 2023, but they are not real market prices and should not be used for any buying or selling decision.
